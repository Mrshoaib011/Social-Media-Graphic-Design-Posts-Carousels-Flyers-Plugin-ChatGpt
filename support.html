Frequently asked questions

How do I get a better first result?

Provide a clear goal, the audience, preferred format, real product image and logo. Specify exact wording for prices, offers, claims and contact details. Missing optional details can be omitted; important product facts should not be invented.

How do matching variations work?

Choose the same product or a different product, then request two, four or another number of additional posts. Variations use fresh headline and supporting copy and a changed main layout. Backgrounds can change within the original theme. CTA, logo and fixed footer content and positions stay consistent.

Can ChatGPT choose the size and style?

Yes. Say “Let ChatGPT decide” for creative preferences such as size, style or composition. This does not authorize invented product claims, prices, contact details or replacement brand assets.

Why did it ask me for a product photo or logo?

The tool uses actual brand assets. If an essential image cannot be retrieved or identified, it may ask you to upload it. A website link does not guarantee access to every image.

Why are text or product details imperfect?

Generative images can contain errors. Ask for a specific correction and check the revised output. The tool cannot guarantee pixel-exact preservation, an exact font, vector output, CMYK color or print-ready bleed without an appropriate export and verification process.

How can I hide asset recommendations?

Say “hide asset recommendations.” The optional affiliate recommendation is separate from the graphic and does not affect your design. Its rolling 24-hour limit works only when the host provides reliable private persistent state; otherwise the automatic footer is skipped.

Where are my files and conversations stored?

Design inputs and outputs are processed in the host you use, such as ChatGPT. This static website does not accept design uploads. Read our Privacy Policy for support data, hosting and third-party processing.
