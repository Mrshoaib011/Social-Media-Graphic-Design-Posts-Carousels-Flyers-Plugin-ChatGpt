1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Social Media Graphic Design: Posts, Carousels & Flyers is an independent design tool created by Shoaib Akhtar (“we”, “us”). This policy covers this informational website and the information you choose to send the creator for support. It also explains how the tool uses the host’s capabilities.

Contact the creator through the LinkedIn profile linked on our Support page. OpenAI, GitHub, LinkedIn and other providers have their own policies for data they process.

2. Visiting this website

This website contains static pages and locally hosted assets. It does not have account registration, a payment checkout, a design-upload form, analytics trackers, advertising pixels or operator-set cookies. Its code does not write to browser local storage.

When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub may process other technical information under its own Privacy Statement. See GitHub Pages data collection.

3. Prompts, photos and generated designs

When you use the design tool, your prompts, uploaded product photos, logos and outputs are handled by your host and any capabilities you authorize, such as ChatGPT image generation or website retrieval. The current plugin package has no creator-operated MCP server, upload endpoint or separate design database that receives your conversations.

The bundled template library is used for design guidance. It is not a database of user uploads. The creator does not receive your ChatGPT conversations merely because you use this skills-only package. Do not include sensitive or confidential information unless you are authorized and comfortable with the host’s processing.

Host storage, retention, model-improvement settings and account controls are governed by the host. For ChatGPT, consult OpenAI’s Privacy Policy and your account’s data controls. We cannot delete or change records held by an independent provider on your behalf.

4. Information you send for support

If you contact the creator, we receive the profile or contact information visible in your message, the message contents, and any screenshots or files you choose to attach. We use that information to answer your question, investigate issues, manage privacy requests and keep relevant support records.

Provide only what is needed and remove client information, passwords and other sensitive details. Messages sent through LinkedIn are also processed under LinkedIn’s Privacy Policy. We do not sell support messages or use them for targeted advertising.

5. Optional affiliate recommendations

The tool may recommend Creative Fabrica through a clearly disclosed affiliate link after a completed design. Following the link is optional. The creator may receive commission from eligible signups at no additional cost to you.

The tool does not automatically open the referral link or send your prompts, images or contact details to Creative Fabrica. If you click, Creative Fabrica may receive referral and technical information and use its own tracking technologies under its policies. You can say “hide asset recommendations” to opt out.

Where supported, the delivery guard stores only a last-display timestamp and an opt-out flag in private persistent storage provided by the host and scoped to the current user. It does not store identity, conversation contents or analytics. The automatic footer is suppressed when reliable isolated storage is unavailable. This website does not run that guard or store those preferences.

6. Retention, sharing and security

We keep support information only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain relevant support history or meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion of information controlled by the creator. Independent platforms may retain their own copies under their policies.

We may disclose support information when required by law or when necessary to protect rights, investigate abuse or resolve an issue with your authorization. Services used for hosting and communications may process information in countries other than your own. No transmission or storage method can guarantee complete security.

7. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may be entitled to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact the creator using Support and identify the information involved. We may need proportionate verification before acting on a request; do not send identity documents unless an appropriate secure method has been agreed.

Manage ChatGPT conversations and host preferences through your host account. Manage GitHub or LinkedIn data through those services. You may also contact your local data protection authority where applicable.

8. Children

This tool and website are intended for business users and creators, not children under 13. Use of a host platform is subject to that platform’s age requirements, which may be higher in your country. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to the creator, contact Support so we can review and remove information we control as appropriate.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website, tool or data practices change. The date below identifies this version. If a creator-operated backend, analytics, payments or other new processing is introduced, this policy must be updated before those features are enabled.
